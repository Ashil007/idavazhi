Routes, times and fares are estimates

Vazhi builds trips from OpenStreetMap data, published timetables and times added by riders. Buses can be late, cancelled, re-routed or missing from the map, and stops can move. Check with the conductor, station or official sources when it matters.

Vazhi is independent. It is not run by, or connected with, KSRTC, private bus operators, Indian Railways, IRCTC, Kochi Metro Rail Ltd or Kochi Water Metro, and their names are used only to describe their services.

The stop alarm is a helper, not a guarantee

The alarm depends on your phone: its GPS signal, battery, volume and silent settings, battery saving, and whether the browser keeps Vazhi running. Any of these can delay or stop the alarm. Please don’t rely on it alone, especially at night or on an unfamiliar route.

What you add

Bus times, stop names and answers you add are shared with other riders. By adding them you allow Vazhi to store, change and show them. Please add only what you believe is true. Messages must not be abusive, unlawful or spam. I may remove anything that breaks these rules.

Fair use

Don’t try to break, overload or get around Vazhi’s limits, copy its data in bulk with automated tools, or use it for anything unlawful. Vazhi may slow down or block traffic that does.
