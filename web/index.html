Before you start

Vazhi needs two permissions to work. You can't plan a trip without them.

  • LocationFinds the stop nearest you and follows your bus, so Vazhi knows when your stop is near.
  • Alarms (notifications)Wake you before your stop, even when the screen is off or another app is open.

You can change this later in your phone's settings.
