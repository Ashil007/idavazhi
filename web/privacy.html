Privacy policy

Vazhi has no accounts, no ads and no trackers. This page says exactly what it keeps, what it sends where, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

  • You don't sign up. Vazhi doesn't know your name unless you type it into a message.
  • Your saved places, recent trips and settings stay on your phone.
  • During a trip, your live position is used on your phone for the alarm. It is not sent anywhere, unless you turn on “Share where this train is”.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no cookies from Vazhi, and nothing is sold.
  • Payments go through Razorpay. Vazhi never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

What stays on your phone

These are kept in your browser's storage on your own device. I can't see them, and clearing Vazhi's site data (or uninstalling the app) removes them:

  • saved places and recent searches;
  • alarm settings, light or dark map, and similar choices;
  • messages waiting to be sent when you were offline;
  • a random code made by your browser, used so that each phone gets one vote on a bus time. It contains nothing about you.

What reaches Vazhi's server, and why

Planning a trip and searching places

To find a route, your phone sends the start and end points, any stops on the way and the way of travel (bus, train, metro, walking, two-wheeler). Place names you type are sent to find matching places. These are used to answer you and are not stored. The server log records only that a plan was made, with the time and whether it worked: no places, coordinates or addresses.

Your location

If you allow it, your phone's location is used as the start of a trip. It is sent only as part of that trip request. While a trip alarm is running, your position is checked on your phone and never leaves it.

“Share where this train is” (off unless you turn it on)

On a train trip you can choose to help riders waiting for your train. Then, while the alarm runs and your phone is on that train's track, its position is sent about every 45 seconds with the train number and a random code made for that one trip, so two phones aren't counted as one. Nothing else is sent: no name, no device id. Positions are held only in the server's memory for 20 minutes and never written to disk. Others see where the train was last seen and how many riders shared it, never who.

Bus times, stops and places you add

When you add a bus time, rename or add a stop, add a missing place, or answer a question about one, I keep what you added, when, and a scrambled (hashed) form of your phone's random code, so the same phone can't vote twice. What you add is shown to other riders.

Messages and trip ratings

When you send a message or rate a trip, I keep the text, the stars, the name or contact you chose to type (optional), which page it came from, and when. Messages are passed to me through Telegram so I can read them on my phone.

"Buy me a bus ticket" payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. You give your phone number and payment details to Razorpay, not to me. I receive and keep the Razorpay payment and order numbers, the amount and the time, which I need for my accounts. Razorpay's own privacy policy covers what it keeps.

Your internet address

Your IP address is held briefly in the server's memory to stop any one visitor from overloading Vazhi (for example, too many messages in an hour). It is not written to disk or kept.

Other services Vazhi uses

  • Razorpay takes payments, only when you choose to buy a ticket.
  • Telegram delivers riders' messages, ratings and ticket notices to me.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • OpenStreetMap services (map data, place search and road routing) are asked by Vazhi's server for the points or place names involved. Your IP address and identity are not passed on.
  • The hosting provider that runs the server, and the network in front of it, carry the traffic.

How long things are kept

  • Trip plans and searches: not kept.
  • Shared train positions: 20 minutes, in memory only.
  • Messages and ratings: until I have dealt with them, or until you ask me to delete them.
  • Bus times, stop names and added places: as long as they help riders.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (up to 8 years).

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can raise a complaint. Because Vazhi has no accounts, tell me enough to find it: the words of your message and roughly when you sent it, or your Razorpay payment number. I will reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

For grievances about this policy, the contact person is {{OWNER}}, through the contact page.

Children

Anyone can use Vazhi to plan a trip. If you are under 18, please don't buy a ticket or type personal details into a message without a parent's okay.

Keeping it safe

Vazhi is served over HTTPS, collects as little as it can, and checks each payment with Razorpay's signature before counting it. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, I will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be pointed out on the website.
