Refunds and cancellations

How refunds work for “Buy me a bus ticket” payments.

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

  • A bus ticket is a one-time, voluntary payment to support Idavazhi.
  • Paid by mistake, the wrong amount or twice? Tell me within 7 days for a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the way you paid, usually in 5 to 7 working days.
  • Nothing is shipped, and there is no subscription to cancel.

What you are paying for

“Buy me a bus ticket” lets you send {{OWNER}}, the maker of Idavazhi, a small voluntary amount to help keep Idavazhi free. You don’t receive goods, a service or a ticket for any bus, train or metro in return. Payments are taken by Razorpay.

When you can get a refund

You get a full refund if, within 7 days of paying, you tell me that:

  • you paid by mistake, or meant to pay a different amount;
  • you were charged more than once for the same ticket; or
  • Idavazhi said the payment failed but money was taken.

Outside these cases, contributions are not refundable, since nothing is delivered that could be returned. I may still refund in other situations at my discretion.

How to ask

Use the contact page and include the Razorpay payment number from the email or SMS Razorpay sent you (it starts with “pay_”), or the date, time and amount. {{CONTACT_LINE}}

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are started within 2 working days and go back through Razorpay to the card, UPI account, bank or wallet you paid with. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the money. Refunds are not paid in cash or to a different account.

Failed payments

If money left your account but the payment failed, your bank or Razorpay normally reverses it automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If it doesn’t, contact me with the details and I’ll help trace it.

Cancellations

Each ticket is a single payment. There is no subscription, repeat charge or order to cancel.

Shipping and delivery

Idavazhi doesn’t sell or ship physical goods. Nothing is delivered after you pay, apart from a thank-you on screen.
